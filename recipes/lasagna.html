Classic Italian Lasagna

A delicious Italian classic made with layers of tender pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese.

This homemade lasagna is baked until golden and bubbly, making it the perfect comfort food for a family dinner or a special occasion.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 700ml tomato sauce
  • 1 onion,finely chopped
  • 500ml béchamel sauce
  • 200g grated mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Prepare the sauce:

    2. Heat the olive oil in a pan. Add the chopped onion and cook until soft.

  2. Cook the meat:

    3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.

  3. Add the tomato sauce:

    4. Stir in the tomato sauce, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 20 minutes.

  4. Prepare the oven:

    5. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).

  5. Assemble the lasagna:

    6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish. Add lasagna sheets, béchamel sauce, mozzarella and Parmesan. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used.

  6. Finish the top layer:

    7. Cover the final pasta layer with meat sauce, béchamel, mozzarella, and Parmesan.

  7. Bake:

    8. Cook for 35-40 minutes, until the top is golden and bubbling.

  8. Let it rest:

    9. Allow the lasagna to rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Enjoy your homemade Italian lasagna!
