Eggplant Parmesan (Melanzane alla Parmigiana)

A traditional Italian dish made with layers of tender fried eggplant, rich tomato sauce, mozzarella and grated Parmesan cheese.

Baked until golden and bubbling, this comforting recipe is perfect for a family meal and bring the authentic flavors of Italian cuisine to your table.

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants
  • 700ml tomato sauce
  • 250g mozzarella cheese
  • 100g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare the eggplants:

    2. Wash the eggplants and slice them into thin rounds. Sprinkle with salt and let them rest for 20-30 minutes, then pat them dry.

  2. Prepare the tomato sauce:

    3. Heat the olive oil in a pan. Add the chopped onion and cook until soft. Pour in the tomato sauce, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 15-20 minutes.

  3. Fry the eggplants:

    4. Heat the vegetable oil in a frying pan. Fry the eggplant slices until golden on both sides, then place them on paper towels to remove excess oil.

  4. Preheat the oven:

    5. Set the oven to 180°C (350°F)

  5. Assemble the dish:

    6. Spread a layer of tomato sauce in a baking dish. Add a layer of eggplant slices, mozzarella, grated Parmesan, and fresh basil. Repeat until all the ingredients are used.

  6. Add the final layer:

    7. Cover the top with tomato sauce, mozzarella and grated Parmesan.

  7. Bake:

    8. Bake for 30-35 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden.

  8. Let it rest:

    9. Allow the dish to rest for 10-15 minutes before serving.

Enjoy your homemade Italian Eggplant Parmesan!
