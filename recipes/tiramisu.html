Classic Italian Tiramisù

A traditional Italian dessert made with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and a smooth, creamy mascarpone filling.

Topped with a dusting of cocoa powder, this delicious no-bake dessert is rich, light, and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 300g ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi)
  • 500g mascarpone cheese
  • 4 fresh eggs, separated
  • 100ggranulated sugar
  • 300mlstrong espresso coffee, cooled
  • Cocoa powder, unsweetened for dusting
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Prepare the coffee:

    2. Brew the espresso coffee and let it cool completely.

  2. Separate the eggs:

    3. Carefully separate the egg yolks from the egg whites into two different bowls.

  3. Prepare the cream:

    4. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar until the mixture becomes pale and creamy. Add the mascarpone cheese and mix until smooth.
