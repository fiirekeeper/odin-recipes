Tiramisù

Classic Italian Tiramisù

A traditional Italian dessert made with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and a smooth, creamy mascarpone filling.

Topped with a dusting of cocoa powder, this delicious no-bake dessert is rich, light, and perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 300g ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi)
  • 500g mascarpone cheese
  • 4 fresh eggs, separated
  • 100g granulated sugar
  • 300ml strong espresso coffee, cooled
  • Cocoa powder, unsweetened for dusting
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Prepare the coffee:

    2. Brew the espresso coffee and let it cool completely.

  2. Separate the eggs:

    3. Carefully separate the egg yolks from the egg whites into two different bowls.

  3. Prepare the cream:

    4. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar until the mixture becomes pale and creamy. Add the mascarpone cheese and mix until smooth.

  4. Whip the egg whites:

    5. Add a pinch of salt to the egg whites and beat until stiff peaks form.

  5. Combine the mixtures:

    6. Gently fold the whipped egg whites into the mascarpone cream until fully combined.

  6. Dip the ladyfingers:

    7. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the cooled coffee. Do not soak them for too long, or they will become too soft.

  7. Create the layers:

    8. Arrange a layer of coffee-soaked ladyfingers in a baking dish. Spread half of the mascarpone cream over them. Repeat with another layer of ladyfingers and the remaining cream.

  8. Add the cocoa powder:

    9. Dust the top generously with unsweetened cocoa powder.

  9. Refrigerate:

    10. Cover and chill in the refrigerator for at least 4-6 hours, preferably overnight.

  10. Serve:

    11. Cut into portions and serve chilled.

Enjoy your homemade Italian Tiramisù!
